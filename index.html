Privacy Policy

App: Market - Shop Business Simulator

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Mohammad Kasim] handles information when you use Market - Shop Business Simulator.

1. Information Collection

Market is a single-player simulation game. The game does not require users to create an account or provide their name, phone number, or email address to play.

Game progress, including levels, in-game cash, stock, shop upgrades, settings and daily targets, may be stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

This application uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Advertisement views and interactions
  • App interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention and related services.

For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Users can voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to receive virtual in-game rewards.

Rewards may include:

  • Temporary free access to selected shop facilities.
  • Free in-game cash.
  • Additional shop stock.

These rewards are virtual and have no real-world monetary value. Some rewards are time-limited and subject to in-game conditions.

Advertisements may not always be available due to internet connectivity, device compatibility or advertising provider availability.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information collected through Google AdMob may be shared with Google and its service providers for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention purposes.

5. In-App Purchases

The current version of Market does not offer in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid game services.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally on the user's device. It generally remains on the device until the user clears the application data, resets the game where supported, or uninstalls the application.

We do not operate our own game servers or account database for this single-player release.

Advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to its own privacy and data retention policies.

7. Data Security

We do not maintain a personal user account database. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

8. Children's Privacy

The application does not intentionally collect personal information directly from children.

Parents or guardians who have privacy concerns may contact us using the email address provided below.

The developer must ensure that the application's actual target audience and advertising configuration comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

This application uses Google Mobile Ads SDK. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. User Choices

  • Users can choose whether to watch rewarded advertisements.
  • Users can manage their advertising ID through Android settings.
  • Users can clear locally stored game data.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
